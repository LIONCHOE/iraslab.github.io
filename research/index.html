01

Radar Imaging

Radar image formation and advanced signal processing

Pipeline 01 → 02 · signal to trustworthy representation

How can incomplete and distorted echoes be converted into accurate, interpretable images and signatures?

Research image to be added

Raw pulse processing, range-Doppler mapping, autofocus phase error correction, and high-resolution SAR/ISAR image reconstruction.

This domain turns raw radar returns into trustworthy intermediate representations: SAR/ISAR formation, motion compensation, autofocus, 3D rotational compensation and cross-range scaling, super-resolution, and polarimetric or interferometric scattering analysis.

We move beyond the monostatic limit through bistatic, multistatic and MIMO interferometric ISAR geometries and W-band synchronization across distributed platforms, recovering resolution from sparse apertures via compressive sensing and deep super-resolution.

Frequency band and platform are secondary. Work belongs here when its main contribution is synchronization, correction, reconstruction or resolution — whatever the application.

Intelligent Target Detection & Recognition

Robust radar perception and target intelligence

Pipeline 03 · representation to target meaning

How can targets be detected, identified and rejected as unknown under clutter and limited training data?

Research image to be added

Deep CNN/Transformer target recognition (SAR-ATR) and open-set unknown target rejection from multi-input radar representations (SAR chip, HRRP, micro-Doppler).

This domain converts representations into target intelligence, spanning CFAR, change detection and maritime small-target detection through SAR-ATR, NCTR, micro-Doppler drone recognition, and open-set / OOD decisions.

The central problem is performance that survives deployment: lightweight on-board SAR-ATR for embedded platforms, feature-level clutter reduction for domain generalization (IRASNet), and residual standardization that flags targets never seen in training.

Deep learning is a method, not a separate domain; it is combined with scattering centers, HRRP, RCS and statistical processing as needed, and with SAR–IR–vision fusion where one sensor is not enough.

Radar Systems, Electronic Warfare & Electromagnetics

Mission-aware radar systems and electromagnetic operations

Pipeline 04 ↺ 01 · closed-loop optimization

How should radar systems be modelled, protected and operated under contested electromagnetic and resource constraints?

Research image to be added

Electromagnetic PO/PTD RCS scattering simulation, chaff cloud dispersion, radar jamming (DRFM/RGPO) M&S, and active phased-array beam scheduling loop.

Electronic attack and protection, jamming M&S, RCS and low-observable analysis, passive radar and clutter modelling are unified by the operational electromagnetic environment. We model the monopulse seeker of a threat missile down to its sum/difference patterns, then quantify how noise jamming, RGPO, cross-eye, chaff and decoys change intercept performance.

Engagement analysis is only as good as its electromagnetic modelling: RCS of large composite stealth platforms via high-frequency asymptotics (PO, PTD, SBR), dominant-scatterer diagnosis, and RCS prediction for million-scale chaff clouds through coupled aerodynamic–electromagnetic analysis.

Multifunction radar prioritization and scheduling close the loop from perception back to waveform, beam and time allocation. Evaluation here favours engagement effectiveness, resource efficiency and electromagnetic robustness over single-shot detection accuracy.

Human-Centered RF Sensing & Smart Spaces

Privacy-aware sensing of people, activity and physiology

Pipeline 01 → 04 end-to-end · no SAR/ISAR dependency

How accurately and robustly can presence, location, activity and physiology be sensed without recording optical imagery?

Research image to be added

Contactless real-time respiration/heart-rate phase extraction, smart indoor 3D human pose localization, and crowd people counting density estimation.

IR-UWB, MIMO FMCW, CW radar and RF signals are used to estimate occupancy, people count, indoor location and activity under clutter, multipath, posture change and subject variation — evolving from scattering-center extraction through feature-based learning to spatiotemporal transformers.

Physiological sensing extracts respiration, heart rate and blink duration from phase time series, optionally strengthened by video–RF feature fusion, and detects lying-down people whose returns are weak through their respiration signal.

Radar is lighting-independent and records no optical imagery, so it delivers privacy-aware indoor information. SAR/ISAR imagery is not a prerequisite here.

Autonomous Radar & Multimodal Perception

Robust 3D perception for mobility and robotics

Pipeline 01 → 04 end-to-end · multimodal fusion

How can vehicles and robots understand 3D environments reliably in adverse weather and complex clutter?

Research image to be added

Automotive FMCW angular super-resolution & 4D point clouds, road clutter suppression, VRU tracking, and Radar-LiDAR-Vision fused occupancy perception.

Automotive FMCW and 4D imaging radar research covers angular super-resolution, unambiguous range extension, radar cubes and dense point clouds. The end metric is reliable perception and tracking, not image formation itself.

We suppress road, guardrail and multipath clutter in adverse weather and track vehicles and vulnerable road users, fusing radar, LiDAR and cameras to reconstruct 3D environments and occupancy.

Non-mechanical solid-state LiDAR simulation and comparative sensor-capability studies extend the work naturally from vehicles to robotics and autonomous platforms.
