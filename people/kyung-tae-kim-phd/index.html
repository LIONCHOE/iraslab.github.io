Research Area

  • Radar Imaging — Radar signal modeling; Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) / Inverse SAR (ISAR) imaging; High resolution radar signal processing; Time-frequency analysis
  • Radar Target Recognition — Non-Cooperative Target Recognition (NCTR); Automatic Target Recognition (ATR); Pattern Recognition
  • Automotive Radar — Signal processing algorithms to detect multiple targets
  • Polarimetric Radar Signal Processing — Polarimetric SAR calibration; SAR antenna area constraint; Radiometric resolution
  • Radar Cross Section — EM Theory and Numerical analysis (GO, GTD, PO, PTD, SBR, etc.); RCS measurements
  • Array Antenna Synthesis & Beam Pattern Optimization — Antenna beamforming; Array pattern optimization
  • Advanced Signal Processing — Independent component analysis (ICA); Empirical Mode Decomposition (EMD); Compressive Sensing (CS)
  • Micro-Doppler — Micro-Doppler of radar targets
